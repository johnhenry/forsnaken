🐍 Forsnaken 🐍

Photosensitivity warning. This game flashes the screen when a snake eats an apple. A very small percentage of people may have seizures when exposed to flashing lights. If you or anyone in your family has epilepsy, consult a doctor before playing, and stop immediately if you feel dizzy or unwell.

Green: arrow keys, the first controller's d-pad, or swipe. Yellow: WASD or the second controller. Each has a light twin that mirrors it, and the white snake steers itself. Space pauses, R restarts.
